## What it is

Hearthside is a private household hub for you and your partner. It gives you one clear view of how your home is doing, month by month and year by year.

## Highlights

- **One clear picture.** Your household at a glance.
- **Monthly and yearly views.** See trends over time.
- **Built for two.** Shared by default, with room for privacy.
- **Works on any phone.** Install it like an app on iPhone or Android.
